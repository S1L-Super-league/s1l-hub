Start here — the 5 minutes that matter

From players Tested in game From the internet Official source 15 Sep 2026

Almost everything in this game comes down to one idea: spend the resources you were going to spend anyway on the day they count two or three times. Not spending more — spending on the right day.

Every day, no matter what is running
  • Do your Alliance Duel tasks — every day the duel is running. This is where the largest, most reliable share of your points comes from.
  • Collect your duel chests. The milestone rewards sit in the event window until you pick them up. They never arrive on their own, and an uncollected chest is the same as no chest.
  • Play Turbo Turtle up to 10 million when it runs and you can reach it. That is the top milestone — it pays the prestige weapon fragment.
  • Finish your daily tasks and collect the milestone chests.
  • Collect both free 50-stamina packs. Always — they are free and they expire.
  • Take the free gathering.
  • Give alliance help and donate to alliance tech.
  • Keep a garrison on a goldmine. The mine runs permanently, not as an event — gold comes in while you do other things.

These cost nothing, do not depend on winning anything, and are the most reliable points in the game.

Stamina and Intel Missions — do not just burn them

Stamina: collecting the two free packs is the daily habit. Spending extra stamina out of your backpack belongs on duel day 1, where stamina scores duel points — that is the day it counts twice.

Intel Missions: the duel rewards them on days 1, 3 and 5 and on no other day, so there is no reason to run them in between. On any day, do the level 10 wandering infected — the first kill of the day carries the rewards that matter. Beyond that, either save your Intel Missions for the next scoring day, or run only as many as a special event actually needs.

The four mistakes that cost the most
  • Spending your stacks on the wrong day. Speed-ups, gear material, hero material and other stockpiles pay most on the day VS or Turbo Turtle scores them. Spent on any other day, the same stack scores nothing.
  • Treating Power Play as a third big event. It is a small bonus you take along when it happens to fit — not something to plan your week around.
  • Sitting unshielded on duel day 6. A weak unshielded base is free kill points for the enemy.
  • Attacking a NAP6 alliance or anything marked on the map. NAP6 is our non-aggression pact with the six top alliances of our state, us included. The current list is posted with the weekly plan on the alliance board. It damages our relationships — and we need allies for Arcadia. If in doubt, ask first.
One habit that separates good players from strong ones

Before you spend a stack of anything, ask: is there a day this week where this same action pays twice? Nine times out of ten there is. That is the whole game.

Building: the power plant sets the pace

  • The power plant is your core building. Its level unlocks the next level of all other buildings, and each power plant level needs certain other buildings first — e.g. power plant 30 needs research lab 29, barracks 29 and war herald 26.
  • Do not let research fall behind. The lab gives lasting bonuses — building and training speed, gathering, and your duel point bonus. A power plant rushed without research is strong on paper only. (players: cosx, official server)
  • Power plant 26 is a big step: it unlocks troop level 9. (player: Nick #190, official server)
  • Time your upgrades: finish buildings on duel day 2 and research on duel day 3 — see The week at a glance.

Heroes: few, but properly

  • Focus on squad 1 and raise it as a whole instead of spreading fragments and manuals across many heroes. Rarity: gold (SSR) above purple (SR) above blue.
  • Every hero has a type (icon on the card, e.g. shield or gun) and belongs to one of three factions — the three tabs in the hero list (mountain, sands, sky). The Honor Expedition battlefields match these factions, and reforged stats must fit the type.
  • Faction bonus: put heroes of the same faction into one squad. The game shows it when you tap your heroes where they stand on the training grounds in your settlement (Edit march), then the faction icon. 2 of a kind give +2% final stats, 3 give +8%, 4 give +18%, 5 give +25% (from 3+2 or 4 on also +5% counter-attack damage). Faction counters (mountain → sands → sky → mountain) add 20% damage; sea-voyage heroes count as every faction.
  • Which heroes are worth it, and how prestige gear helps everyone: see Heroes and troops.

Troops and healing

  • Train on duel day 5 — training scores duel points there. Keep your barracks busy the rest of the week only if you are short of troops.
  • The healing trick: start short healing jobs of 1–2 hours and ask for alliance help — with enough helpers the timer runs out almost at once. Do it together twice a day and your troops are back without speed-ups. (player: AnesiShark, official server)
  • Where troops really die: in Oil / Copper Clash lost troops are gone (power lost). In Reservoir Raid they are only wounded and heal for free. So never trade troops against a much stronger enemy in a clash. (players: FAMBAT, Nick #190, official server)
  • Before you dismiss troops, read the warning in Heroes and troops.

Gems, VIP and free gems

  • Log in every day. The daily login gives VIP points, and the amount grows with your streak (on day 79 it is 650 points a day). Collect the free daily VIP chest.
  • VIP 8 gives an extra march slot and more hero attack. Experienced players advise putting spare gems into VIP levels before one-off items. (VIP screen in game; advice: cosx, official server)
  • Free gems: in the 3v3 Arena you can like the top 100 commanders up to 3 times a day — 30 gems per like. Following an official community channel once gives 500 gems (Events → Community).
  • Buy only in the game or in the official FunPlus top-up centre — see Where to look things up.

Alliance basics

  • Every day: alliance help, a tech donation, and your daily tasks. Help costs you nothing and saves others hours.
  • NAP6: we do not attack the six top alliances of our state. The current list is posted with the weekly plan.
  • Pooling boosts: when R4 puts a marker on one settlement, everybody sends their building or research boost there — one player then finishes a long upgrade in hours instead of days. (player: Feline, official server)
  • For R4: the alliance skills for gathering and infected need no new donation, recharge in 23 hours and last 6 hours — switch them on daily (gathering in the morning, infected in the afternoon). (member: Drdr, S1L Discord)

Protection: the peace dome

  • The peace dome (bought in the alliance shop) is the only real protection. Keep it on and renew it before it runs out.
  • Attacking removes your own dome. Relocating does not stop a march that is already on its way.
  • On duel day 6 and around Arcadia: dome on, troops inside, no gathering without a dome.

3v3 Arena 5 free fights a day

Open to everyone with power plant 16 or higher. You set up 3 teams; fights are best of three, and the defender's order is random. Your troops take no damage here. Winning against a higher rank takes over that rank. 5 free challenges a day; daily tasks pay for 1, 3 and 5 challenges. Your arena defence also counts as your defender in the Alliance Siege — set it up.

Season II — Awakening of Dragons

From players Tested in game From the internet Official source (in-game rules and calendar) Season rules read in game (season preview), 28 Sep 2026

Our state is heading into season II. The countdown is in the season preview in your settlement (icon on the right). This is what the rules in game say — and what to do before it starts.

Your goal and the alliance goal
  • You: raise your dragon resistance. Dragon monsters strongly reduce the damage your troops deal — higher resistance cancels that. In a raid you also profit from the raid leader's dragon resistance.
  • The alliance: collect Dragon DNA. It comes from taking cities, the state battle and the Gene Clash, and it decides the final rank of the alliance and of the state.
  • The loop: get season resources (raw fish from the fishery, dragon fruit from the plantation) → raise dragons and the Razorback in your settlement → more dragon resistance → defeat warp dragons → more resources.
  • Cities reset at the start of the season, and warp dragons occupy them. To take them back you need enough dragon resistance.
Before the season starts
  • SR to SSR: in season II the SR hero that can be upgraded is expected to be Sarge. He must be at 10 stars first — save universal SR fragments and upgrade him on the hero development day. (players, official server)
  • Heal and keep troops ready. Season events are troop-hungry; in the Gene Clash, like in the Oil Clash, lost troops are gone.
  • Keep a stack of speed-ups and resources for the first days — the season buildings want both.
  • Read the season rules yourself once it opens: season preview → Saison-Regeln. Details we do not know yet are announced in alliance chat.

How to read it in ten seconds

Find today's column. Everything with a mark in it scores. Everything else is the same spending on a day where it counts for nothing.

A ✕ and a ● in the same cell is the moment to empty a stack. Building speed-ups in column 2, research speed-ups and behemoth cells in column 3, hero material in column 4 — those are the cells that pay twice.

Milestone chests — thresholds and exactly what is inside

Daily tasks feed two things at once: alliance points for the day's race, and your personal milestone chests. The chests run from 50,000 to 5 million points. The number on a chest is its item value; what matters is what is actually in it.

The pattern worth knowing

Every third chest is the good one. The chests at 500k, 1.5M, 3M and 5M hold hero fragments, skill manuals, behemoth cells and gear scraps. The ones in between are gems and resources. If you are deciding whether one more push is worth it, look at which chest is next.

Turbo Turtle — the 7 phases

From players Tested in game From the internet Official source (in-game rules screen) All seven phase lists read action by action from the in-game phase screens, 21–27 Sep 2026

Turbo Turtle turns gear upgrades into event points. Every upgrade pays twice — your stats go up and you score. Blue scraps are worth roughly four times green ones.

Each phase scores a different list of actions. Not just a different topic — a different list. Phase 1 and phase 5 both want hero gear, but phase 5 also wants common parts and precision blueprints while phase 1 wants troop training instead. Improving gear (verbessern) and strengthening it (verstärken) are counted as two separate actions. Read the matrix, not the headline.

The turtle currently runs phase 1 on Monday through phase 7 on Sunday, the same as the duel days. The in-game rules allow 5–8 phases of 1–2 days each, so check the phase counter in the event (for example “Phase 3/7”). The list inside a phase stays the same, and the exact point values appear in game on the day itself.

The full phase list is in the week table

Every action of all seven phases — next to what the duel wants on the same day — is in The week at a glance. It is kept in one place so the two never drift apart.

The 10M milestone and prestige weapons

There is now a milestone at 10 million points — confirmed on the event screen, where the ladder reads 10k / 1.0M / 2.0M / 3.5M / 5.0M / 10.0M. It gives a fragment. The chain from there: 5 fragments → 10 coins, and 20 coins → one new prestige weapon for a hero. Upgrading that weapon costs the same coins, so the coins stay useful long after the first weapon.

"Stage" means two different things — keep them apart

Rank tiers (5): Bronze → Silver → Gold → Diamond → Legend. These decide how rich your milestone rewards are.

Phases (1–7): these decide which actions score today.

How promotion, medals and milestones work

Promotion is by placement in your small group, not by a fixed score. At the end of each phase the best of the group move up: Bronze top 4, Silver / Gold / Diamond top 3. Everyone else holds or drops. Watch your group standing, not only your total.

Three leaderboards run at once: your group (4–5 players, decides promotion), your rank tier, and the overall event ranking which adds up all phases.

Race medals come from the levels you reach. In the shop: 20 medals buy one top gear item once; single medals buy a 1-hour speed-up or a supply box ×10.

Power Play — the 4-hour rounds

From players Tested in game From the internet Official source (in-game rules and calendar) Rounds and calendar read directly from the game

A solo ranking event with rotating themes. Each round lasts 4 hours. Every day at 00:00 UTC twelve players with similar power-plant levels are drawn into a group, and you are ranked inside that group for the day.

Keep this in proportion

Power Play is a bonus you take along when it happens to line up with what you were doing for VS or Turbo Turtle anyway. It is not a third event to plan around, and it is not worth spending resources for on its own.

There is no schedule that fits everyone — open your own calendar

You are grouped by power plant level (seen in game: "Group: Level 26–Lv.27"), and which theme lands in which slot is not the same for everyone any more. There is no alliance-wide timetable worth posting.

Where to look: open Power Play and press the Calendar button at the left of the banner. It lists Date · Activation time · Theme for the coming days and highlights the round running right now — six slots a day, four hours apart. That table is yours; read it there, not here.

Do not confuse it with Turbo Turtle

Power Play runs in 4-hour rounds. Turbo Turtle runs in day-long phases. They are different events and they often reward the same action — which is exactly what makes a Golden Window.

The Golden Window

From players Tested in game From the internet Official source Reasoning from our own notes — not verified end to end

A Golden Window is a moment when one action pays into four things at once: the Alliance Duel day, the Turbo Turtle phase, the matching Power Play round, and your actual progress. The turtle is the one most people forget — and on the right day it is often the biggest of the four, because turtle points come in automatically for something you were going to do anyway.

The clearest example

Building speed-ups, finished on duel day 2 (Base Building), inside a Settlement Construction Power Play round, while Turbo Turtle runs phase 2, 6 or 7. One stack, four payouts.

Those three turtle phases are the only ones that score building speed-ups. If the phase is 1, 3, 4 or 5, the same stack pays three times instead of four — still worth it, just not the peak.

The same logic applies to gear scraps on day 1, research on day 3 and hero material on day 4. Two more overlaps worth knowing:

  • Cave Conquest also awards Alliance Duel points — so playing it on a duel day counts twice.
  • Stamina on duel day 1 feeds the Radar day and, when Sawblade Waltz is running, produces sawblades at the same time.

Which windows are open this week is announced on the alliance board in game. This page deliberately carries no dates.

Oil Clash season event

What wins it: whoever holds the most deep-sea crude at the end. Everything resets at 00:00 UTC — yesterday's ownership protects nothing.

What every member should understand
  • We lose when we do not have enough anchors to attack together. Strength alone is not enough.
  • Half is enough — holding a spot past its threshold is wasted effort.
  • Small players should drop into anything unoccupied immediately. An empty slot produces nothing; an occupied one produces around the clock, even offline.
  • Softening a target counts as much as taking it. If you damage a spot, a big account can walk in afterwards with a single anchor. That saves exactly what we have least of.
  • There is no worthless attack. Even damage that does not kill a hideout still builds the alliance buff.

The threshold formula

Daily threshold = points per minute × 720. Once we pass it, the enemy can no longer catch up that day — and from then on no more anchors go there. Whoever is already sitting stays (sitting is free), we simply stop attacking. If the enemy passes it first, attacking there is pointless.

Two hard limits

Connection rule: you can only attack what borders our own territory — the alliance has to advance as a connected chain. Occupied rule: you cannot slide into an occupied spot, you have to take it. That is why free slots are so valuable.

Staggered unlocking: towers carry countdown locks. Early on most of the map is closed; at the end everything opens at once and everyone fights simultaneously. That is the real reason for anchor discipline.

Anchor economy

Every attack costs one anchor. Resupply is +8 per day, +10 with the season pass, at 00:00 UTC. The cap is 15 — anything above that is lost.

The night trick

After the 00:00 UTC reset everything is free and most opponents are asleep. Dropping into three territories right then collects all night without a single fight. Best effort-to-points ratio in the event, explicitly including small accounts.

Reservoir Raid 30 v 30, cross-state

What wins it: the alliance with the most alliance water when the timer ends. Only water decides the win. Water at a building is not safe yet — as long as it shows above the building, the enemy can steal it. Water from puddles and water tanks is safe and cannot be stolen. Kills give points for the personal ranking, not for the win.

Alliance rule — this part is binding

[S1L] plays in five teams: four colour zones — yellow (top left), orange (top right), red (bottom left), blue (bottom right) — plus the centre team, who move freely. In phase 2 we join two teams into one. Which teams join is announced before the raid. Every team has a leader.

Before every raid:

  • Read the rules on this page first. Every player, every raid. Is your language missing? Just ask — we will add it.
  • Send a thumbs up in your assigned group in good time before the raid — it shows that nothing came up and you will be there. So the leaders can still assign a substitute before the raid starts and it gets stressful.

During the raid:

  • All team leaders are together in voice chat. They decide the plan and post it in the team chats and in the reservoir chat.
  • In the reservoir chat only team leaders write (or players they name) — the most important information for everyone. Everyone else writes there only in an emergency.
  • Your team leader is your contact. Need something or under attack? Say it in your team chat. Your leader is in voice chat with the other leaders.

What a team leader is responsible for:

  • Coordinating with the other team leaders — on Discord, or in chat when speaking is not possible for language reasons.
  • Opening the group and checking that everyone who should be there is there.
  • Asking whether everyone knows what to do — and assigning people if that is needed.

Stay in your assigned zone. Substitutes do not join on their own — they wait for the leaders' instruction in case someone drops out or has to be swapped.

How water works — and how we steal it
  • First capture bonus: the first alliance to take a building gets a one-time bonus. It is safe and cannot be stolen.
  • A building only starts to collect water after it has been held for 2.5 minutes.
  • Stealing: attack and take a building. The water it collected then drops around it as puddles. Whoever collects the puddles keeps the water.
  • Jump to the puddles at once if you have a free teleport — before the enemy gets them.
  • Only selected players from the strong group attack the buildings. Shortly before, the leaders post the coordinates and the time left until the capture.
  • At the end of the raid the water still above a building counts for the alliance that holds it.
Our three game plans — the leaders call the plan

The leaders decide in voice chat and post PLAN 1, PLAN 2 or PLAN 3. The plan can change during the raid. Water tanks are collected in every plan.

  1. PLAN 1 — we are much stronger: we stay in five teams and hold everything.
  2. PLAN 2 — similar strength, or the enemy fights back: we take only the important buildings (map below) and collect and protect our water there. Near the end, on the leaders' call, we attack the other buildings and steal their water.
  3. PLAN 3 — the enemy is stronger: we leave the buildings, hold only the centre and collect water tanks. Then one of two ways: if we are strong enough, we let the enemy fill the buildings and steal the water from the important ones near the end. If we are too weak for that, we attack again and again so that the enemy never holds a building for 2.5 minutes — then it collects nothing. Which way, and the details, the leaders post in the reservoir chat during the raid.
The tasks in each zone

Who does which task is decided by the team leader.

  1. Take our buildings in the zone.
  2. Collect the water tanks in our quarter.
  3. When a building is captured, the water it collected spreads around it — you can see the water on the map around that building. Someone always has to jump there and pick it up.
  4. When the central building shows water on the map and we have already collected well, everyone except the one person securing the main buildings jumps to the middle and collects the puddles.
Two tips: keep your troops, see everything
  • Take your troops off the wall before you enter the raid. Tap your wall and remove the blue check mark at Garrison for every squad. Then you lose no troops when you are attacked in the raid. Only the troops that march with your heroes in your own attacks get wounded. The red warning in this window does not apply in the reservoir raid.
  • Use the magnifier on the small map (top right). Tap it to see the state of every building and all water tanks.

Arcadian Conquest 2-hour siege

What wins it: the alliance that holds Arcadia for one hour without interruption during the two-hour fight — or, if nobody manages that, the one with the longest total occupation at the end. Its leader automatically becomes governor. Holding the towers makes the attacks on Arcadia come more often. Scoring: 10,000 points for every 60 seconds you station troops in Arcadia or in a tower. It runs every week, server against server: one server guards Arcadia, another invades.

Roles, the relocation window and the realistic goal

Relocation window — this one is critical. One hour before the start, settlements in the danger zone around Arcadia are moved to a random spot. From 50 minutes before the start until the end you can move into the danger zone freely, but peace shields in the zone are removed and cannot be switched on again. A settlement defeated inside the zone is moved to a random spot at once.

Roles: only the strongest 5–8 hold the centre. Two or three per tower. The bulk runs rally waves in groups of five teams. Weaker accounts take side objectives — not the centre. Rotate garrisons every 30–60 minutes and put AoE heroes into the garrison.

Realistic goal: one hour of uninterrupted hold on Arcadia, or the longest total hold — not surviving two hours against nobody.

Alliance Siege 5 days · arena line-ups

Not the same as Arcadian Conquest. Open only to alliances in the top 36 of the state with at least 30 commanders who have set an arena defence line-up. Each week six alliances form a group, and every day your alliance faces another one from that group. Both sides take turns attacking the other side's defending commanders — each successful attack earns a victory point.

What every member should do
  • Set your arena defence line-up. Your defender is your arena defence from the day before, or your strongest team in the 3-v-3 arena. Without it you do not count towards the 30.
  • Use your attack every day. Each attacker earns at most one victory point per day, and each defender can be beaten only once — so every member's single hit counts.
  • Stronger defenders give more victory points. One special reinforcement defender can be attacked without limit but gives only a few points.

At 23:55 UTC the alliance with more victory points wins that day's bonus rewards and group points. After five days of fighting, the group ranking decides the weekly rewards and whether the alliance moves up a tier. Daily rewards are claimed on the event screen; group rewards come by mail. New members can only take part from the next Alliance Siege.

Ghoulion Pursuit 30 minutes

An alliance boss rally where your personal damage decides your reward bracket. The hunt lasts 30 minutes. Everyone who can lead a rally leads their own instead of waiting — maximum hits per minute is the whole game here.

Before it starts
  • Switch to single-target and boss-damage heroes, signature weapons and damage gear. Take the tank gear off.
  • Hold your damage buffs and attack consumables for these 30 minutes only.
  • Donate before the event — donations strengthen the hunting field and give battle bonuses. The level resets when the event ends, and donations beyond the maximum level do not carry over.
  • Only R4 or higher can start or schedule the hunt. Raids on the hunting field cost no stamina. Anyone who joined the alliance less than 48 hours ago cannot take part.
Reward brackets

Rewards are paid out at the end by damage: your own damage sets your personal bracket, and the alliance's total damage unlocks extra rewards for every member. The brackets and items grow with the Ghoulion level — read them in game under Rewards.

The top player's damage multiplies the alliance reward: ×1 up to 99.99M, ×2 to 159.99M, ×3 to 239.99M, ×4 from 240M. So pushing the best player higher lifts everyone.

City Battle PvP

The one rule that saves you gems: only the first capture of a city gives the instant bonus. Retaking a city gives none — so do not burn gems on extra march slots for a recapture. It still helps the alliance through the growth bonus and duel points.

The two cases, step by step

Case A — city with monsters. Everyone goes for the small ones first: one march each onto a free small monster, so points spread across the alliance. The big centre and the middle ones only on the commander's call. Once the big one is down to 25%, everyone goes in together.

In more detail: the small ones can already be killed before the event. When the small one showing a percentage hits 50%, new small ones spawn — so clear small ones first, then that one. Then the medium-small ones until the city is at 75%, then down to 50%, and so on. From 25% and once the small ones are gone, the centre.

Case B — city held by an enemy alliance. Everyone hits it together until it is ours, then everyone sends troops in to defend for 30 minutes. After that it is ours.

Always: come to the rally point when R4 calls, and keep watching alliance chat — plans change on the fly.

Cave Conquest also gives duel points

Defeat tunnel puppets, collect sandworm eggs, earn tunnel points and alliance duel points. One-hour blocks alternate between clearing puppets and taking the tunnel. Each alliance can control only one tunnel at a time.

Rewards and the thing people get wrong

Personal milestone chests at 15,000 (manual ×2, 5-min speed-up ×5, supply box ×30), 30,000 (supply box ×40) and 60,000 (coin, manual ×2, hero figure ×1, speed-ups). Control rewards are distributed by R4 and above.

Common misunderstanding: the sandworm eggs are a points collectible, not something that hatches into a behemoth. Behemoths come from cells and serum, not from caves.

Lost Frontier 10 tries a day

Beat 30 normal monsters per difficulty to unlock the next one: Easy → Normal → Hard → Insane → Hell. You get 10 attempts per day, reset at 00:00 UTC. Every fifth monster a supply hoarder appears — beat it with a raid. Use your strongest team, not a fixed formation.

The mistake

Burning attempts on a difficulty you cannot clear. You only unlock the next tier by clearing all 30. Raise heroes first, then push.

The help mechanic — the most common question

Asking for help happens before the raid starts, as an invitation; the rally only appears after the start. Two separate steps.

Find it via the green Help button in the event, or the small double arrows (») at the right edge of the chat banner — not by tapping the banner itself. To help others, look under the Event tab in the rally menu, not among normal rallies.

Honor Expedition resets Tue / Thu / Sat

Three battlefields, each giving +20% to one hero class: Mountain Citadel → Valor heroes, Howling Sands → Rogue heroes, Azure Apex → Flight heroes. Complete the challenge on each for glory coins.

Two rules that decide your whole cycle
  • You pick a difficulty once and cannot change it. Pick the highest you can clear safely — overreaching wastes the cycle.
  • Each hero can only be used on one battlefield per cycle. Spread them deliberately.

Mountain Citadel is the key: the other fields are gated behind its progress — Azure Apex stays locked until Mountain Citadel difficulty 13 is done. Push Mountain Citadel first. Spend glory coins on the best available gear rather than scattering them.

Pet Journey pet event

Bring any pet to the required level or star rating to earn adventure paths. The paths move your paw print along the map and unlock chests on the way. Unclaimed chests are mailed when the event ends — unused paths are not, so spend them before it closes.

The overlap worth planning around

Pet marks and pet food also score on duel day 2. When Pet Journey runs in the same week, do your pet levelling on duel day 2 and it counts twice.

Sawblade Waltz hero event

Spend 50 stamina in the wilderness to get one sawblade. Use sawblades to summon marked elite infected, raid them with allies, and you have a chance at Lucky's shards. Enough shards unlock her.

The overlap worth planning around

Duel day 1 (Radar) rewards spending stamina. That is the same stamina you need for sawblades — so on Radar day, spend it deliberately and it counts twice.

Shard milestones sit on roamer levels 4, 7, 10, 13, 16, 19, 22, 25, 28 and 30. If you want Lucky, chase those. Raids are an alliance matter — say when you summon so it does not go to waste.

Progression Exchange 5 free swaps

A limited-time event that lets you move progress from one hero to another instead of losing it. Two modes:

  • Swap skill levels between two heroes — their maximum skill level has to be identical.
  • Swap prestige gear levels between two heroes.
Why this matters more than it looks

This is the one mechanic that undoes a bad investment. If you levelled skills or prestige gear on a hero you no longer field, the exchange moves that work onto the hero you actually use. You get 5 free attempts — do not let them expire, and think before you spend one, because the swap goes both ways.

Doomsday Express 4 + 4 per day

Send your own trucks and raid other players' vehicles. Four send attempts and four raid attempts per day; delivery takes roughly 2.5 to 3 hours. Collecting a finished delivery does not cost an attempt.

What you get

Your own delivery, collected: 800,000 food · 800,000 wood · 160,000 metal · 40,000 fuel · 15-min speed-ups ×3 · medals ×8 · cells ×2.

Raid rewards by class: A tends toward premium items, B toward large resources, C mostly speed-ups. The preview shows the defender's power before you commit — check it.

Underground Goldmine runs permanently

Not an event — it runs the whole time and is simply a daily habit. Garrison a mine tile and it gathers gold while you do other things. Mines run level 1–6; levels 5 and 6 have a minimum recall time. Rare high-level mines are first come, first served. Do not defend a low-value mine at the cost of two teleports.

Heroes and troops

The three hero tips below were checked in game on the hero screens (28 Sep 2026). The line-up comes from an experienced player on another state and is marked as second hand.

Three heroes whose prestige gear helps everyone

The prestige gear of Rosie, Jacob and Chiron carries a skill that raises the attack and defence of all heroes, not just the hero wearing it — in game: Rosie's guitar at level 3 gives +14% each, Jacob's pistol and Chiron's bow at level 1 give +10% each. Take that into account when you plan which prestige gear to upgrade first — spending there pays across your whole roster.

Chiron's passive works on the whole march

Chiron's passive Penetrating Resonance raises the attack and defence of all allies — at skill level 1 by 2% each, per the skill text in game. Members report that "allies" here means the troops in the march, not other players.

Lucky reduces what the wilderness costs you

Lucky's passive Wild Hunt is a global effect: it lowers your stamina consumption and raises your attack damage. At skill level 10 the game shows −12.5% stamina and +4.3% damage; each level adds a little more.

Upgrade her on the hero development day — the same action then scores duel points as well.

Turning an SR hero into an SSR hero

In some seasons an SR hero can be upgraded to SSR. The hero has to be at 10 stars first. Which hero it is changes with the season: season 1 Lucky, season 2 Sarge, season 3 Chef (players, official server) — check the season screen in game.

After the upgrade the hero restarts at 5 stars and can be taken back to 10 with epic hero fragments — but at double the quantity. Total needed: 2,520 fragments. Worth knowing before you commit, because it is a long road.

Do it on the hero development day and put all your skill manuals into that hero first. The upgrade gives the manuals back — so they score duel points twice and you can use them again on another hero. (player: Feline, official server)

A line-up reported from another state second hand

Passed on from an experienced player on state 48, so not our own measurement: main team Chiron, Layla, Candy, Jacob, Becca; second team Ray, Tarzan, Tara, Rosie, Nikola. Reported to reach roughly 250 million hero strength per march. Useful as a direction, not as a target to copy blindly.

Troops — and a warning about dismissing them

Duel day 5 is the troop day: you score as long as you have the space and the speed-ups to train.

Read this before you dismiss troops to free up space

You reach your troops through the profile window, bottom left. Troops in the settlement can be reduced there.

The settlement window pre-selects every troop for dismissal. Change the number by hand and dismiss in small steps. Dismissed troops lower your combat strength in the alliance ranking — this is one of the few actions in the game you cannot undo.

Gear and reforging

From players Tested in game From the internet Official source (in-game screens) Read from the German game interface, Aug 2026

Reforging rerolls the special values on a piece of gear. The interface shows you the probability before you commit, and the result can be better or worse than what you had. Check the value pool first — the screenshots below show where those numbers are.

How to reforge without wasting it

The value has to fit the hero. An excellent value for precision shooters does nothing on a guardian hero. Rare values are worth more than common ones — but only on the right hero.

Keep rolling until the result is better than what you had, then save. After saving you can carry on rolling and hope for something better still, without risking what you already banked.

The probabilities — and the one rule that follows from them

Each reforge draws from a value pool. The chance of landing a higher tier is:

Common45 %Exquisite30 %Epic20 %Legendary5 %

The game states that the more often you reforge a piece, the higher its chance of getting higher-tier values. So rolling is not a coin flip you repeat — it improves. That is the mechanical reason behind "keep rolling, save when it is better, then roll on".
